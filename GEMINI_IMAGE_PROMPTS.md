# Prompts Gemini — Décors de donjon · Les Cryptes Éternelles

Optimisés pour **Gemini / Imagen** (l'app Gemini ou Google AI Studio). Gemini
préfère des **phrases descriptives naturelles** (comme si tu briefais un peintre)
plutôt que des listes de mots-clés. Les prompts ci-dessous sont en anglais (les
modèles d'image sont meilleurs en anglais), prêts à copier-coller.

---

## 📐 Mode d'emploi (à lire une fois)

1. **Format vertical** : dans l'app Gemini, demande explicitement un format
   portrait. Chaque prompt finit déjà par *"Vertical portrait composition with a
   9:16 aspect ratio"*. Si l'app propose un réglage de ratio, choisis **9:16**.
2. **Cohérence entre les 7 zones** : génère **`cryptes.jpg` en premier**. Puis,
   pour les autres, **joins l'image de `cryptes` en référence** et ajoute au
   début du prompt : *"Match the art style, brushwork and color grading of the
   attached reference image."* Gemini accepte image + texte → la famille
   visuelle restera homogène.
3. **Si du texte apparaît** par erreur, relance en ajoutant : *"Absolutely no
   text, no letters, no symbols, no watermark anywhere in the image."*
4. **Noms de fichiers** : enregistre chaque image avec le nom EXACT du titre de
   section (ex. `cryptes.jpg`) et dépose-la dans `web/images/`.
5. **Phrase d'ancrage de style** (déjà incluse, mais utile à réutiliser) :
   > "A moody dark-fantasy digital matte painting, atmospheric and cinematic,
   > desaturated muted colors, deep shadows, volumetric fog, painterly detailed
   > brushwork, no people and no text."

## 1. `cryptes.jpg` — Les Cryptes Éternelles (PRIORITÉ : zone finale)

> Create a moody dark-fantasy digital matte painting of a vast, ancient
> underground crypt — the final eternal tomb where a forgotten god lies sealed.
> Towering blackened stone pillars carved with faintly glowing crimson runes
> recede into deep darkness on either side. Down the center, a long processional
> path of cracked, worn flagstones leads the eye toward a distant stone altar
> bathed in a faint blood-red glow. Heavy iron chains hang from an unseen
> ceiling, shattered sarcophagi lie along the walls, and fine dust and embers
> drift slowly through the air. The mood is oppressive, sacred and silent, like
> a tomb that has waited a hundred years. Use a palette of near-black shadows
> with deep crimson accents, a single eerie red light source far ahead, and soft
> volumetric fog. Cinematic, atmospheric, painterly and highly detailed. No
> people, no characters, no text or symbols. Vertical portrait composition with
> a 9:16 aspect ratio.

## 2. `marais.jpg` — Le Marais Putride

> Create a moody dark-fantasy digital matte painting of a fetid, haunted swamp
> at dusk. Twisted, leafless dead trees rise out of black, stagnant water, their
> roots draped in hanging moss. A thick, low-lying green mist clings to the
> surface, lit from within by pale glowing wisps and patches of bioluminescent
> fungi. A half-sunken stone causeway winds away from the viewer into the
> distance, leading toward the silhouette of a rotting, ruined shrine on the
> horizon. The water bubbles faintly with decay. The mood is sickly, damp and
> eerie. Use a palette of putrid greens and cold teal over near-black water,
> with a sickly luminous haze. Cinematic, atmospheric, painterly and highly
> detailed. No people, no characters, no text or symbols. Vertical portrait
> composition with a 9:16 aspect ratio.

## 3. `volcan.jpg` — Le Volcan Ardent (Caldera d'Aszgol)

> Create a moody dark-fantasy digital matte painting of the throat of an active
> volcano — a hellish caldera. Rivers of molten orange lava cut glowing veins
> through jagged black basalt rock. Cracked fissures radiate searing light,
> ash and floating embers rise through the air, and a smoke-choked deep-red sky
> looms overhead. A narrow obsidian ledge forms a path that climbs away from the
> viewer toward a distant fiery summit. Shimmering heat haze distorts the air and
> cinders fall like snow. The mood is intense, infernal and dangerous. Use a
> palette of deep black rock against searing orange and red glows, with heavy
> volumetric smoke. Cinematic, atmospheric, painterly and highly detailed. No
> people, no characters, no text or symbols. Vertical portrait composition with
> a 9:16 aspect ratio.

## 4. `chateau_maudit.jpg` — Le Château Maudit (Acte IV)

> Create a moody dark-fantasy digital matte painting of the interior of a cursed
> gothic castle, once grand and now decayed. Towering broken stone arches and
> cracked columns frame the scene, tattered banners hang from above, and dusty,
> cobwebbed chandeliers hold guttering ghostly candles. A sweeping ruined
> staircase climbs away from the viewer into deep shadow. Cold pale moonlight
> pours through a shattered stained-glass window, casting long blue-tinted
> shafts of light across the rubble-strewn floor. The mood is haunted, spectral

> violet-black with cold blue moonlight. Cinematic, atmospheric, painterly and
> highly detailed. No people, no characters, no text or symbols. Vertical
> portrait composition with a 9:16 aspect ratio.

## 7. `cryptes_profondes.jpg` — Les Cryptes Profondes (Acte IV · finale ultime)

> Create a moody dark-fantasy digital matte painting of the deepest heart of the
> eternal crypts — the abyssal chamber where the God of Memory is sealed. At the
> far end of an immense black chamber stands a colossal ancient sealing door,
> covered in dimly pulsing crimson runes that glow like a slow heartbeat. Vast
> iron chains anchor the door into the surrounding rock, and a single stone altar
> waits before it. Fine ash drifts through crushing darkness, lit only by the
> faint red pulse. The mood is overwhelming dread, sacred finality, the end of a
> long journey. Use a palette of pure black with deep blood-crimson accents and
> heavy volumetric fog, an ominous red rim light outlining the immense door.
> Cinematic, epic, atmospheric, painterly and highly detailed. No people, no
> characters, no text or symbols. Vertical portrait composition with a 9:16
> aspect ratio.
